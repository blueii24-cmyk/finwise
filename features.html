Features

Everything you need to understand and organise your money.

Income Tracking

Note down pocket money, stipends or salary so you know what you start the month with.

Expense Tracking

Record daily spending by category, from food and travel to books and fun.

Budget Planning

Set limits for each category and see how much money is left.

Savings Goals

Choose a target, such as a laptop, and estimate how many months it will take.

Financial Overview

See your income, expenses and remaining balance in one simple dashboard.

Simple Guidance

Use clear totals and savings estimates to make better everyday choices.
